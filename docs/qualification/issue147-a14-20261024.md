commands and 14 equal before/after preservation inventories:

  preview and complete restoration under a new generation-1 output identity.

  configuration and authority records during reader operations.

  Reader capture and revocation refused at the administrator boundary.

- Malformed head, tampered artifact and revoked stale preview refusal before
  destination creation.

Every candidate/fixture command ran inside a network-denying sandbox. One
explicit pre-provisioning OS-only `sudo -n /usr/bin/true` reader policy probe

attachment used an inherited Unix datagram socket whose peer discarded every
frame: 87 frames / 25,165 bytes discarded, no forwarding or uplink. Guest-agent
control used the separate virtualization transport. There were no host shares,
clipboard/audio/USB passthrough or provider credentials. The guest shut down
with exit status zero after evidence export. Existing outputs were not inputs.

The fixture was intentionally prospective. Source and restored outputs were
created inside this disposable guest. The protected head was revoked by the
last negative control; these test resources are not operational authority.
